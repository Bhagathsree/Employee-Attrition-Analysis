# 📊 Employee Attrition Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the major challenges faced by organizations, as high employee turnover can lead to increased recruitment costs, reduced productivity, and loss of experienced talent. This project analyzes the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset using Python to identify factors associated with employee attrition through data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), multivariate analysis, and data visualization.

The project demonstrates how Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn can be used to transform raw HR data into meaningful business insights that support better workforce management and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Perform data preprocessing and cleaning.
- Conduct Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Identify relationships between employee attributes and attrition.
- Create meaningful visualizations for better understanding.
- Generate business insights to support employee retention strategies.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Employees working overtime showed higher attrition than employees who did not work overtime.
- The Sales department experienced the highest employee attrition.
- Sales Representatives recorded the highest attrition among all job roles.
- Employees with lower monthly income were more likely to leave the organization.
- Employees with shorter organizational tenure showed higher attrition.
- Job Level and Monthly Income exhibited a strong positive correlation.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Monitor overtime to reduce employee burnout.
- Improve employee engagement and work-life balance.
- Review salary structures to retain talented employees.
- Strengthen career development opportunities.
- Focus on retention strategies for high-attrition job roles.
